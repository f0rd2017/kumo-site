Privacy policy

Kumo · last updated October 2, 2026

What Kumo is

Kumo is a desktop application for personal use that stores the user's own files in the user's own Google Drive accounts, encrypted on the user's device.

Data Kumo accesses

  • Google Drive, drive.file scope only. Kumo creates a "Kumo" folder in your Drive and reads, writes and deletes only the files it created there. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address (openid, email) — only to show which account is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • Your files are compressed and encrypted on your computer (XChaCha20-Poly1305) before they are uploaded. The encryption key is derived from your password and never leaves your device unencrypted. Google stores only encrypted data that cannot be read without your password.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on your device and inside your encrypted Kumo index.
  • Kumo has no server. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party; Kumo communicates only with Google's APIs on your behalf.
  • Kumo does not use your data for advertising, analytics, or training of any models, and does not sell or share it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kumo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Deleting files in Kumo deletes their encrypted parts from your Drive. You can remove everything by deleting the "Kumo" folder in Google Drive and revoking Kumo's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
