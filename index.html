Kumo

A personal, end-to-end encrypted cloud file explorer on top of your own Google Drive accounts.

🔒

Encrypted on your device

Files are chunked, compressed where it helps and encrypted before upload. Google stores only unreadable data.

🧩

Spread across accounts

Chunks are distributed evenly across every Google Drive account you connect.

🗂️

A real file manager

Native desktop app with grid and list views, previews, a video player and a text editor.

🙈

Sees only its own files

Access is limited to the drive.file scope. No server, no developer backend.
